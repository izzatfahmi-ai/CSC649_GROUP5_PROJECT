# Volleyball Knowledge Base — Data Source Note

**Dataset File:** `data/volleyball_kb.csv` (60 Q&A entries)  
**Project:** VolleyBot

## Where the Data Came From
- **Official Rules & Match Procedures:** Adapted from the **Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB) Official Volleyball Rules** for indoor 6-on-6 volleyball (scoring, court dimensions, net heights, rotations, touches, faults, timeouts, substitutions, misconduct cards, and key indoor vs. beach volleyball differences).
- **Skills, Positions & Tactics:** Compiled from standard volleyball coaching terminology covering player roles, serve types, passing and digging mechanics, attack tempos, and offensive rotation systems (`4-2`, `5-1`, and `6-2`).
- **Dataset Formatting:** Curated and written into structured `id,topic,question,answer,tags` rows for local retrieval in VolleyBot.
